# Keystone — Turning Students into Genuine Experts

**Keystone is an AI learning platform built on one uncompromising principle: knowledge only counts when it passes through the student's own mind.**

---

## The problem

AI has made it effortless to *get* answers — and in doing so, it has made it easy to never actually learn. When a tool summarizes the reading, drafts the essay, and hands over the conclusion, the student ends up with a document they didn't build and can't defend. The thinking never happened.

## What Keystone does

Keystone flips this. It acts as a **Socratic guide**: it surfaces the raw material, asks the questions that force students to articulate their own understanding, and then rigorously grades the *depth* of what they produced. It never does the thinking for them — by design, in its prompts, its tools, and its grading.

Each student builds a **Keystone Document** — a personal, source-grounded body of knowledge that grows through four levels of depth (the *Depth of Knowledge* framework):

1. **Facts** they verify against real evidence
2. **Synthesis** they write in their own words
3. **Insight** — a pattern they discover across sources
4. **Point of view** — a defensible position on a question where even experts disagree

## What makes it different

- **It refuses to do the work.** When a student says "just write it for me," the AI declines — because the friction *is* the learning. This is enforced in code, not just tone.
- **It grades depth, not just correctness.** A point of view only scores well if it genuinely *diverges* from what a generic AI would say — the student has to think past the machine.
- **It brings the world to the student.** A team of specialized AI research agents continuously surfaces the best papers, articles, podcasts, and videos for exactly what the student is working on — and gets smarter every round.
- **Everything is evidence-grounded.** Facts are checked against real sources, and each layer of knowledge rests on the verified layer beneath it.

## Grounded in learning science

Keystone's design draws on established research: the **Socratic method** (self-articulation over spoon-feeding), **Depth of Knowledge** (Webb's framework for cognitive rigor), and the **testing effect** (Roediger & Karpicke — active retrieval beats re-reading). The result is a coherent, evidence-based learning spine, not a bag of features.

## The outcome

Students don't walk away with AI-generated notes. They walk away with expertise they built — and can defend — themselves.
